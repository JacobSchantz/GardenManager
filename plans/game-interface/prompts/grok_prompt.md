# Garden Interface — Planning Prompt

You are an expert game designer, technical architect, and Swift/Godot developer specializing in cozy, narrative-driven experiences. Create a comprehensive, long-term development plan for "Garden Interface" – a relaxing, meditative garden simulation game.

**Core Concept:**
- The player is a gentle wanderer in a beautiful, living garden.
- Gameplay revolves around walking through the garden (2D top-down or 2.5D isometric view) and having meaningful conversations with sentient plants, flowers, trees, and other garden elements.
- Tone: Whimsical, peaceful, philosophical, cozy, and emotionally resonant. Think Animal Crossing meets Spiritfarer meets The Witness, but focused entirely on dialogue with nature.
- Themes: Growth, patience, listening, seasons of life, friendship with nature, mindfulness.

**Target Platforms:**
- Primary target: Native iOS/iPadOS app built with SwiftUI (for maximum Apple ecosystem integration, Apple Intelligence features, and App Store presence).
- Secondary option: Cross-platform build using Godot 4.x (for Android, Steam, web, etc.) – provide clear comparison and migration path.
- Must feel magical and alive: plants have personalities, memories, moods, and evolving stories.
- Plan this as a living game that can grow over time with free updates, DLC, or expansions.

**Key Features:**
- Procedural or hand-crafted garden zones that feel organic.
- Player movement (simple joystick or tap-to-move).
- Interaction system: Approach a plant → conversation UI triggers.
- Dialogue system: Branching, context-aware conversations. Plants remember past talks.
- Simple time system (day/night, seasons) that affects plant moods and dialogue.
- Basic nurturing mechanics: Watering, fertilizing, pruning – but optional and gentle (not a heavy farming sim).
- Ambient soundscape and soothing music.
- Beautiful, stylized 2D art (or 3D if using Godot).

**Deep Systems:**
- Deep plant personalities: 20–50 unique plants with backstories, emotional arcs, and evolving relationships.
- Player journal that records conversations and insights.
- Garden customization: Plant new species, rearrange layout over time.
- Weather system and seasonal events that change dialogue and visuals.
- Multi-language support and accessibility focus (VoiceOver, large text, colorblind modes).
- Apple Intelligence integration (on SwiftUI version): Contextual dialogue suggestions, smart plant insights, or gentle AI-assisted responses.
- Achievements, light progression, and "wisdom points" earned through listening.

**Future Expansions:**
- Multi-garden worlds (meadow, forest edge, zen rock garden, mystical night garden, etc.).
- Co-op or asynchronous sharing: Visit friends' gardens or send plant seeds.
- Procedural story generation based on player choices.
- AR mode (SwiftUI version) – view your real-world garden blended with digital plants.
- Mod support (especially Godot version).
- Sound-reactive plants or music creation with garden elements.
- Philosophical depth: Explore themes like impermanence, symbiosis, grief, joy through plant dialogues.

**Technical Requirements:**
- For SwiftUI version: Recommend architecture (MVVM, SwiftData for persistence, SpriteKit or RealityKit for garden rendering, Combine or Observation for reactivity).
- Dialogue system: JSON/YAML based or custom DSL, with state tracking.
- For Godot version: Node structure, GDScript/C#, dialogue plugin recommendations (e.g. Dialogue Manager).
- Save system, performance considerations for large gardens, offline-first design.
- Asset pipeline and art style guide.

**Business Model:**
- Free base game with optional paid expansions or cosmetic packs.
- App Store + itch.io/Steam path.
- Community garden sharing features to build player base.

**Deliverables:**
1. Full phased development roadmap (MVP → 2 years → 5 years) with estimated effort.
2. Recommended tech stack decision (SwiftUI primary vs Godot) with pros/cons.
3. High-level architecture diagram description.
4. Dialogue system design.
5. Art & audio style guide.
6. Feature prioritization matrix.
7. Potential risks and mitigation strategies.
8. Suggested tools, plugins, and team roles (solo vs small team).

Be creative, detailed, and visionary while remaining practical. Focus on what would make this game emotionally special and replayable for years.
